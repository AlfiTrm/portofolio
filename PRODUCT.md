# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

This portfolio primarily speaks to two audiences: recruiters or hiring stakeholders who need to quickly judge execution quality, and potential clients or collaborators who want confidence that Alfi is capable and easy to work with.

They usually arrive with limited time and incomplete context. They need fast proof that the work is real, the taste is intentional, and the person behind it can contribute reliably in a team or project setting.

## Product Purpose

This site exists to turn a personal portfolio into a strong professional signal. It should help visitors understand Alfi's level, see evidence of real work, and feel comfortable reaching out for hiring or collaboration.

Success means visitors leave with the impression that the work is serious, the execution is thoughtful, and Alfi is someone worth contacting for a role, freelance build, or creative collaboration.

## Positioning

A personal portfolio for a frontend developer who feels capable, polished, and genuinely good to work with.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary and secondary CTA: The primary action is reaching out to connect for work or collaboration. The secondary actions are reviewing featured projects and opening the resume for validation.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: This is someone capable, intentional, and worth talking to for real frontend work.
- Belief ladder: The site should first feel visually credible, then reveal real shipped work, then show enough range and care to make contact feel justified.
- Proof on hand: Existing project and experience references already present in the repo, including [src/features/home/projects/data/projectsData.ts](/D:/Project_Alfi/porto-gwh/portofolio-alfi/src/features/home/projects/data/projectsData.ts), [src/features/home/about/data/timelineData.ts](/D:/Project_Alfi/porto-gwh/portofolio-alfi/src/features/home/about/data/timelineData.ts), and the recognition surface in [src/features/home/skills/data/skillsData.ts](/D:/Project_Alfi/porto-gwh/portofolio-alfi/src/features/home/skills/data/skillsData.ts).

## Brand Personality

The brand should feel clean, elegant, and distinctive. It should read as personal without becoming casual, and polished without becoming stiff or corporate.

Emotionally, the site should create confidence first, then curiosity. Visitors should feel that the work has taste, but also discipline.

## Anti-references

This should not feel like a generic AI-made portfolio, a noisy experimentation playground, or an overly formal corporate personal site.

Avoid anything that reads too crowded, too cold, too childish, or too template-driven. The site should stay visually memorable without becoming loud.

## Design Principles

1. Show capability through proof, not inflated self-description.
2. Let clean composition and whitespace create confidence.
3. Keep the portfolio personal and distinctive, but still employable.
4. Make smooth motion reinforce polish, never distract from credibility.
5. Every major section should help a visitor move from impression to trust.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readability is a hard requirement. Responsive behavior is non-negotiable across desktop and mobile. Motion should feel smooth, but always remain controlled and compatible with reduced-motion expectations. Contrast should stay strong enough that elegance never comes at the cost of clarity.
